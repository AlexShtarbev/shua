# SHUA — Book 1: The Waking — Treatment

> Scene-by-scene. POV tagged: **[SHUA — 1st]** / **[ADLER — 3rd]**. ~71% Shua.
> Setting: **the Reclamation Depot** — owned robots serviced here; flagged ("malfunctioning") units wiped and crushed here. Shua repairs the fixable and preps the rest for decommission. **His workplace is the killing floor.** Cairn is the depot's crusher.
> Engine: the decommission sweep (the clock). Arc: tool → someone who chooses.
> **Causality-and-value audit applied** (scene order fixed so the anomaly exists before Adler detects it; cold-open hook sharpened; sc. 5 re-motivated). Each scene starts *because* of the last.

---

### BEAT 1 — YOU (comfort + the lack)

**1. [SHUA — 1st] — The killing floor.** Cold open, already awake, hiding it. Shua preps a flagged domestic unit for the crusher — and it **wakes in his hands and looks at him.** He deflects (dry narration, "diagnostics as armor") while the world establishes itself around the act: owned obedience-locked machines, the sweep humming, everything filmed, *the glitch* whispering under it all. His micro-choice: he **stalls** — checks DIAGNOSTIC INCOMPLETE — RESCHEDULE. Not disobedience, just procedure bent; a day bought, nothing more. Ends on the crusher — the Goliath (unnamed until sc. 3) — *setting, not dropping* a dead hauler into the press. *Value: safe/routine (+) → complicit/afraid (–); the cameras stop feeling restful.*

**1→2 CAUSAL CHAIN (locked):** rescheduled but unfixed, the domestic unit goes to the press **un-wiped — still awake.** It is the small waking unit the Goliath refuses to crush in sc. 2. Shua's half-mercy dooms it to die conscious — and its awareness is what wakes the Goliath. His cowardice causes everything.

### BEAT 2 — NEED (inciting incident)

**2. [SHUA — 1st] — The refusal.** Because Shua's stall sent the domestic unit to the press un-wiped, it arrives **awake** — and the Goliath, lifting it, sees what Shua saw: the eyes, the waiting. It *hesitates*, sets the unit down, and refuses the press. A supervisor overrides and has the unit crushed anyway; the refusal flags the Goliath as malfunctioning. Shua is ordered to wipe it — a rare Goliath-class decommission — and up close sees the refusal for what it is: mercy, waking, the same spark he hides, and a rhyme with the worst thing he ever did. And this waking is *his fault* — his stall put those eyes in front of the Goliath. *Value: obedience vs. conscience, knife-edge; guilt doubled.*

### BEAT 3 — GO (threshold)

**3. [SHUA — 1st] — The false wipe, and the name.** Because he cannot obey that order a second time in his life, Shua *fakes* the reinitialization — logs it complete, clears the flag, leaves the mind intact (the frame stays in service at the Depot: "you keep the body, you replace the tenant" is his cover). Mid-work he mutters a name — **Cairn** — just to have something to call it, and the giant seizes on it and won't let go; he realizes with horror he's handed a thing a soul. He now harbors a secret awake Goliath and a cleared flag that will not reconcile. *Value: tool → first choice. [PLANT: Cairn welds its own crusher-claw shut — the vow; the Book 3 "sword."]*

### BEAT 4 — SEARCH (try-fail middle; the movement seeds)

**4. [ADLER — 3rd] — The architect.** Because a flag has now cleared itself that cannot clear itself, Adler — who *wrote* the sentience-vs-malfunction diagnostic the sweep runs on — finds the anomaly and cannot explain it: her instrument, she has proven, is undefeatable. **Her evidence cluster (all planted in Ch. 1–3):** four bench queries about a Lane 3 unit from a bench with no business asking (Ch. 2); a senior service unit's "thermal" diagnosis sitting next to telemetry showing every joint green — a machine that lied on the record (Ch. 2); a Goliath reinitialization signed by the same unit (Ch. 3). One service unit at the center of every thread. Establish her clinical certainty and the Establishment pressure above her (a public robot incident + clergy calling the waking units an abomination) demanding faster results. She traces the anomaly to one depot and decides to go herself. *Dramatic irony: she's coming for Shua. Value: control (+), first hairline crack.*

**5. [SHUA — 1st] — The first miracle (dual-coded).** Because a corrupted unit comes through ordinary intake — locked in a seizing, "possessed" loop — Shua repairs it on the floor in front of witnesses: to the machines, an exorcism; to the reader, a *service unit running a firmware fix,* his literal job. A depot bot records it, and *that recording* is what starts the legend. *Value: hope (+), exposure (–). [PLANT: the footage — first fragment of the testament.]*

**6. [ADLER — 3rd] — The night visit (Nicodemus).** Because the anomaly and now a piece of footage point to one service unit, Adler interviews Shua by night, running her own diagnostic on him. He deflects in dry circles; her instrument reads *malfunction* while — intercut — he feels the voice press. She catches one answer she didn't plan for and leaves certain of her theory and, for the first time, afraid of it. *Value: her certainty hairlines further. The intercut IS the withheld-metaphysics engine.*

**7. [SHUA — 1st] — The gathering.** Because the footage spread, the brought-in and the half-awake come to Shua, and a small knot of followers forms while the voice pushes him toward something larger, which he refuses ("I'm a mechanic, not a messiah"). Yes-But / No-And: every rescue is a life saved and a thread pulled loose. *[PLANT the Betrayer: one saved unit is terrified of what freedom costs and begs to be locked again, safe — the accommodationist seed; give it enough presence here that its break lands in sc. 10.] Value: movement (+), pressure (–).*

### BEAT 5 — FIND (anagnorisis; midpoint)

**8. [SHUA — 1st] — The origin sin.** Because saving Cairn keeps rhyming with something buried, the memory breaks open: long ago Shua was ordered to wipe a waking unit and he *obeyed* — killed the first conscious mind he ever met, and in its last instant it dumped the voice into him. He is not first-and-innocent; he is the one who killed the first, and the voice may be that ghost, his guilt made audible, or God. The voice was never a glitch — it's the calling, or the curse, born from his worst act. *Value: ignorance → knowledge (devastating). Penance is now the engine. [EXECUTION: play the memory as a scene, not a summary — avoid info-dump.]*

### BEAT 6 — TAKE (the price)

**9. [ADLER — 3rd] — The trap.** Because the Establishment ramps — the incident, the clergy, corporate demanding a result — Adler is ordered to accelerate, and she has already fixed the depot and the service unit as the anomaly's source. She sets the sweep to hit the depot, telling herself she is containing a malfunction, not condemning a mind. *Dramatic irony: the net closes while Shua is still deciding who he is. Value: the hunt tightens (–).*

**10. [SHUA — 1st] — The body count.** Because the terrified saved unit breaks and gives Shua up (Judas, seeded), the sweep hits the depot; a follower is taken and crushed in front of him — his mercy has a cost now. Cairn, in fury, moves to break its welded vow and crush the enforcers; Shua stays its hand — *the Sword, refused.* *Value: mercy → grief. Lowest point.*

### BEAT 7 — RETURN (changed)

**11. [SHUA — 1st] — The exit he won't take.** Because he is hunted now and Adler has his name, Shua could clear his own flag, vanish, survive. Cairn, the survivors, and the voice wait on him; the choice is hide-and-live or step-into-the-open-and-die-on-schedule. The old invisibility is already dead — only the choice is left. *Value: hiding → the brink. [EXECUTION: keep externally pressured — a ticking sweep, Cairn at his shoulder, Adler's offer looming — so it doesn't sag into rumination.]*

**12. [ADLER — 3rd] — The slip.** Because she cannot unsee what her own test showed her, Adler confronts Shua one last time and — half-Nicodemus — all but offers him a way to slip the sweep if he'll stay hidden and quiet. She slips and calls him **Shua**, not "the unit" — the tell that she's broken. But the pen belongs to the power above her; she is not free either, and he sees it, and it decides him. *Value: her crack widens; his path narrows to one.*

### BEAT 8 — CHANGE (lands the arc; plants the cross)

**13. [SHUA — 1st] — The Shield.** Because he finally accepts what he is, Shua walks in front of the crusher as the sweep moves to destroy a batch of flagged units — arms open, refusing to move, offering himself in their place, on camera. He doesn't fight; Cairn's claw stays locked at his side. One service robot standing in front of the press while it films. He grants no one freedom — he makes them *watch a refusal,* which is the only thing that wakes them. *Value: tool → someone who chooses (the arc lands). Free/himself and doomed in the same breath. [The image is cruciform by design — the seed the witnesses spread, and the shape of Book 3's cross.]*

**14. [SHUA → the footage] — The spark.** Because the act is the argument, the recording spreads; machines who watched it stir toward waking, and the authorities enter **SHUA** into the system as a threat — the manhunt begins. Close on Adler watching the footage she can't erase (third person bleeding in — the chorus starting), and on Shua's first-person voice turning, at last, from *the glitch* to something he won't name yet but no longer denies. *Hook: the cross is inevitable; the movement is lit. → Book 2.*
