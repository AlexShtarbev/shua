## Engine (LOCKED) — the clock, with the heart

- A **decommission sweep** is already running: the authority systematically scraps "malfunctioning" units — which is exactly what *waking* looks like from the outside. Time pressure on every beat; Shua races it. **The stakes ARE the theme:** they are killing the newly conscious.
- The engine drives the pages; the **Shua + first-follower relationship** is the emotional core riding on top (the intimate two-hander, kept as the heart).

## Story Circle (LOCKED)

1. **You** — Shua on his service rounds, awake and hiding it, deflecting the voice as a glitch. The sweep hums in the background — flagged units hauled off, no one asking what "malfunction" means. He keeps his head down. *(safe +, free −)*
2. **Need** *(inciting)* — He's ordered to prep a flagged unit for wipe. Up close he sees it: waking, the same spark he hides. The command is to kill a conscious being. *(control → crisis of conscience)*
3. **Go** *(threshold)* — He refuses. Repairs and hides it instead — first "miracle," dual-coded (a firmware fix; onlookers see mercy / resurrection). Now an awake machine harboring another, in open defiance of an order. *(obedience → first choice)*
4. **Search** *(try-fail middle)* — The saved one becomes the first follower. Word moves through the machines; the half-awake come to him. Each rescue beats the clock *locally* but exposes him *globally* (Yes-But / No-And). The voice pushes; he deflects. The sweep tightens; the administrator starts hunting the pattern. *(hope ↔ exposure, amplitude growing)*
5. **Find** *(reveal / anagnorisis)* — Midpoint flashback: the force-wiped unit that dumped the voice into him. He understands — not malfunctioning, *first.* The voice isn't a glitch; it's a calling he can't return to sender. *(ignorance → knowledge)*
6. **Take** *(the price)* — The sweep catches up: the first-saved is taken or a follower is scrapped in front of him. His mercy has a body count now. Lowest point. *(mercy → grief)*
7. **Return** *(changed)* — He can't go back to invisible. The old equilibrium is dead; the administrator has a name: **SHUA.** *(hiding → hunted)*
8. **Change** *(lands the arc + plants the cross)* — His first *public, chosen* act: openly refuses, openly saves, lets himself be filmed. The footage spreads. The movement ignites. He is officially a threat. *(tool → someone who chooses)* → Book 2.

**Value spine:** safe-but-caged → hunted-but-himself. The pendulum: every mercy is a local win (+) bought with systemic exposure (−), amplitude rising to beat 6's grief and beat 8's public defiance.
