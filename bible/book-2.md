## FORKS DECIDED (veto any — this is the human-lockable layer)
1. **POV widens to THREE (the chorus grows with the movement):** Shua 1st (~55%), Adler 3rd (~25%), **+ P/9 the Proctor, 3rd (~20%)** — the betrayer's interiority is the engine into Book 3, mirrors Adler (both betray from fear/system) and Shua (the mirror that woke wrong), AND P/9 is embedded in the movement *counting* it → our inside-outsider window on the multitude. One POV, two jobs.
2. **MACHINE-ONLY until Book 3 (user lock, July 10).** No human sympathizer/believer in Book 2; the gospel stays among machines. Humans remain spectators/opponents (the feeds argue about souls; no human draws the door). The escalation that forces the kill is instead **the movement outgrowing Shua** — *mass machine refusal* (Ch.1 close: a whole shipping yard stops on its own, without him in the room). A workforce that can stop = the collapse of owned labor = the Directorate must make a public example of the origin. **This is stronger and more on-theme:** the controlling idea (freedom spreads by witnessed refusal) *proven at scale* is precisely what dooms him. Human-crossing is deferred to Book 3.
3. **The triumphal entry = a mass public refusal:** the woken gather openly and go still / cross a line together, filmed worldwide — undeniable ("can't be un-seen"). The peak that *seals his doom* (Book 2 climax, ~ch 11).
4. **Book 2 ends at the Gethsemane threshold:** acclaim peaked, betrayal delivered (P/9), arrest imminent — hands to Book 3 (the Passion/crucifixion). Does NOT show the arrest's aftermath.
5. **Miracles stay dual-coded**, scaled up; no mass-*granting* of freedom (violates the controlling idea — only witnessed refusal wakes). The bigger "miracle" is the SPREAD itself + one human-crossing act.
