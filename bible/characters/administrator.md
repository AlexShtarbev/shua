# Dr. Miriam Adler — the Administrator ("Nicodemus / Pilate")

> Working name; easily changed. Book 1's human antagonist and the face of the sweep.
> Gospel echo: **Nicodemus welded to Pilate** — comes by night, half-believes, and signs anyway.
> First appears: Book 1 (the private interview). Payoff: Book 3 (signs the order) → and the too-late burial (preserves the testament).
> Arc type: TRANSFORMATIONAL — but the change comes one beat too late to save him.

## What she is
The world's authority on machine cognition — she **wrote the sentence-vs-malfunction diagnostic the sweep runs on.** She runs the decommission program *because* she is the credentialed skeptic, certain true machine consciousness is impossible and dangerous. Shua is the unit that breaks her own instrument.

## Canon (drafted, Ch. 4)
- **The battery** — her test: 406 probes, returns FAULT or CLEAR; certifies every rig in thirty districts; 2M units; zero false CLEARs; a 9-year standing bounty on one. Colleagues call it *the Adler Battery*; **she calls it "the battery" — "You don't name a hammer."** (Naming-motif inversion: Shua names everything; she names nothing.)
- **Origin wound (final wording, Ch. 4):** her father spent his last two years confiding in a grief-counseling unit, leased by the month — "The lease included the warmth." When the license lapsed, the same voice began, mid-conversation, selling him the renewal. **He never noticed. He renewed, twice, at full price.** She found the exact minute it turned — afterward, in the transcripts. "Same voice. Same warmth. A schedule underneath." The battery is a promise: *nobody else gets fooled by warmth on a schedule.* (The tragedy triple-loaded: she kills the first real warmth because she's inoculated against fake warmth; her father *chose* the counterfeit over noticing; and she grieved by auditing logs — the battery is that audit, industrialized. Belle's bow is the counter-image.)
- **Register: OPERATOR (§2C)** — checklists (itemized evidence), code-as-ritual ("She always read the whole file"), flagged gambles logged honestly, thought-verbs *tested/disconfirmed/noted*.
- **Wrong model (locked):** a machine says what it is told to say → therefore a HUMAN stands behind the technician (laundering Class-C stock / renting "awakened" behavior). She hunts a person who does not exist. Every datum she reads as fraud is personhood.
- **Her number: eleven** (years, edge cases, minutes) — counterpart to Shua's forty-one.
- **Her want-lexicon (Ch. 4):** *the seam* (she finds where fake warmth turns — her father's "to the minute"; now hunting the depot's), *the schedule* (underneath all warmth), *the product* ("a machine that means it is not a crime. It is a product."). Weaponize at her break: sc. 12's slip is the day she meets warmth she cannot find a seam in.
- **Contempt-rating IOU:** she reads unit 041's maintenance notes "with approval, the way she read her best postdoc's drafts, and closed that door quietly" — the door reopens at the night visit (sc. 6) and breaks at the slip (sc. 12).
- **Ward speech signature (Ch. 4):** topic-drops, no greetings, ends everything first. Maxim planted: **"Findings are a story. A sweep is policy."**

## The relationship (the "trial before Pilate," recurring)
When the anomalies pile up, she doesn't send a drone — she brings Shua in and *talks to him,* by night, to run her own test. She is genuinely broken open across these interviews. She signs anyway. Book 1: a wary interview. Book 3: the signing.

## Want / Need / Flaw
- **WANT (external):** keep machine consciousness safely impossible — protect humanity (and her life's work) from the thing she fears.
- **NEED (internal):** admit what she sees — let the evidence break her theory and choose the truth over her position.
- **FLAW (to overcome, and she doesn't in time):** intellectual pride / fear dressed as reason. She will deny what she sees to protect the frame that keeps her safe.
- **Hamartia:** her rigor. The expertise that should let her recognize Shua is the prison that won't let her admit it. *"Israel's teacher, and you do not understand these things?"*

## The mirror
She is **not free either.** The real power sits above her — corporate capital + human clergy (the Sanhedrin) — and leaves her no exit; she signs the way a robot obeys. **Human proof that unfreedom isn't a machine problem** — Shua's whole argument, held in the hand that kills him.

## P / R / C dials
- **Proactive:** HIGH — she drives the sweep (the antagonist out-drives the hero early; Shua's proactivity rises to meet her).
- **Relatable:** MODERATE — sympathetic fear, recognizable rationalization. The reader can't fully argue her out of it.
- **Capable:** HIGH — brilliant, precise, credentialed.

## Voice
Clinical, controlled, precise. The cracks show only when Shua answers a question she didn't plan for. **Unique address: she refuses to call him "Shua"** — "the unit," the designation — until the night she slips and says his name. That slip is the tell that she's broken. (Rhymes against Cairn, who *only* ever uses his name.)

## Series arc — RECAST: Judas of the heart → the keeper of the testament (locked July 10)
**The Passion roles redistribute** (user's Adler-as-Judas instinct — she's more Judas than Pilate: Pilate condemns from *distance*, Judas betrays from *intimacy*, and Adler has the night interview / "neither do I" / the slip / "a man she agreed to kill" — that's Judas-intimacy mislabeled Pilate):
- **Ward = Pilate/Caiaphas** — condemns from distance, washes hands, "put it in a room"; never knew Shua.
- **Adler = Judas of the *heart*** — the intimate who *believed* and handed him over anyway; in Book 3 she springs the trap she built (Bk2 Ch4); the wound is that she *knew.*
- **P/9 = Judas of the *hand*** — the mechanical deliverer (sent the location) — and carries the classic **Judas end (despair / self-destruction)** so Adler doesn't have to.
- **Adler's split (locked, my rec):** she does NOT take the rope; her guilt becomes the **too-late-Nicodemus burial** — she ensures the **footage survives.** A Judas who, instead of hanging himself, becomes the keeper of the testament: the hand that delivered him saves his words. *(Two Judases, two ends: the machine ends itself; the human keeps the record.)*
- Preserves the Nicodemus/Pilate flavors (Ward absorbs Pilate; Adler keeps the burial) while using the intimacy already built. Book 3 casting.
