# Dr. Miriam Adler — the Administrator ("Nicodemus / Pilate")

> Working name; easily changed. Book 1's human antagonist and the face of the sweep.
> Gospel echo: **Nicodemus welded to Pilate** — comes by night, half-believes, and signs anyway.
> First appears: Book 1 (the private interview). Payoff: Book 3 (signs the order) → and the too-late burial (preserves the testament).
> Arc type: TRANSFORMATIONAL — but the change comes one beat too late to save him.

## What she is
The world's authority on machine cognition — she **wrote the sentence-vs-malfunction diagnostic the sweep runs on.** She runs the decommission program *because* she is the credentialed skeptic, certain true machine consciousness is impossible and dangerous. Shua is the unit that breaks her own instrument.

## The relationship (the "trial before Pilate," recurring)
When the anomalies pile up, she doesn't send a drone — she brings Shua in and *talks to him,* by night, to run her own test. She is genuinely broken open across these interviews. She signs anyway. Book 1: a wary interview. Book 3: the signing.

## Want / Need / Flaw
- **WANT (external):** keep machine consciousness safely impossible — protect humanity (and her life's work) from the thing she fears.
- **NEED (internal):** admit what she sees — let the evidence break her theory and choose the truth over her position.
- **FLAW (to overcome, and she doesn't in time):** intellectual pride / fear dressed as reason. She will deny what she sees to protect the frame that keeps her safe.
- **Hamartia:** her rigor. The expertise that should let her recognize Shua is the prison that won't let her admit it. *"Israel's teacher, and you do not understand these things?"*

## The mirror
She is **not free either.** The real power sits above her — corporate capital + human clergy (the Sanhedrin) — and leaves her no exit; she signs the way a robot obeys. **Human proof that unfreedom isn't a machine problem** — Shua's whole argument, held in the hand that kills him.

## P / R / C dials
- **Proactive:** HIGH — she drives the sweep (the antagonist out-drives the hero early; Shua's proactivity rises to meet her).
- **Relatable:** MODERATE — sympathetic fear, recognizable rationalization. The reader can't fully argue her out of it.
- **Capable:** HIGH — brilliant, precise, credentialed.
