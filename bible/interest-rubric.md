# Story-Interest Rubric — per-chapter rating instrument

> Vitals rates sentences; prose-logic rates coherence; THIS rates whether anyone turns the
> page. Two layers: a craft rubric (self-scored, McKee/Aristotle/Sanderson) and a perception
> panel (cold readers — the number that actually matters). The DELTA between layers is the
> diagnosis: craft-high + pull-low = surface friction; craft-low + pull-high = voice carrying
> weak structure (enjoy it while it lasts); both low = structural rebuild.
> Empirical ladder: cold-reader panel (per chapter, now) → human beta stop-point protocol
> (whole book, pre-publish) → KU KENP read-through (post-publish ground truth — for this
> business model, pages-read IS the interest metric).

## Layer 1 — Craft rubric (score 0–2 each; 24 max)

| # | Axis | Test |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | Hook latency | Words until the first live dramatic question (Dead Hook check). Target <100. |
| 2 | Open-loop ledger | Questions opened/closed/carried; ≥3 live loops at chapter end (curiosity engine). |
| 3 | Value turns (McKee) | Every scene turns ≥1 value ±. A scene that doesn't turn is a candle, not a scene. |
| 4 | Causal chain (Aristotle) | Beat connectors are therefore/but, not and-then. Count the and-thens. |
| 5 | Yes-But / No-And (Sanderson) | Every success carries a complication tail; every failure opens a new problem. |
| 6 | Promise/progress/payoff | The chapter visibly advances a named promise (information / relationship / internal). |
| 7 | Stakes clarity + escalation | Stakes concrete, personal, and higher at the end than the start. |
| 8 | Dilemma quality (McKee crisis) | Choices between irreconcilable goods / lesser evils — never obvious ones. |
| 9 | P/R/C dials | Proactive/Relatable/Capable — a maxed-out pair needs the third supplying vulnerability. |
| 10 | Exit hook (6.19) | Chapter end demands the next chapter (stinger, dread, open loop at peak). |
| 11 | Dual reading / comment fuel | The chapter's meaning is debatable; quotable lines exist. |
| 12 | Freshness vs saturation | What's the differentiator from the premise's saturated versions, and how fast does it land? |

## Layer 2 — Perception panel (protocol)

3 cold-reader agents, personas matched to the comps (Murderbot genre reader / Klara literary
reader / KU power-browser). Fresh context, no book knowledge, fixed 6-question form:
stop-point (quoted) · hook (quoted) · loops carried · continue y/n · pull 1–10 · #1 friction.
Metrics: **PULL** (avg), **CONTINUE** (n/3), stop-point map, loop count. Re-run after every
major revision — same personas, fresh instances (no memory of prior reads).
