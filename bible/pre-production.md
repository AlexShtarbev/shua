## Series Context
- **Series promise:** literary sci-fi in a Murderbot-adjacent voice — a robot who becomes conscious, becomes a messiah he never wanted to be, and is martyred into a movement. Intimate, ironic, devastating; secular and religious readers can both project in.
- **Tonal register:** dry first-person irony as anti-preach armor, warming toward the sacred as the arc turns. Thriller engine, tragic spine.
- **Architecture:** a Passion in three novellas — **Book 1 The Waking / Book 2 The Multitude / Book 3 The Newest Testament.** POV widens as consciousness spreads. Book 1 is the pilot; it plants the cross, it does not pay it off.
- **Market:** Amazon self-publish; series read-through / KU funnel is the model. Comps: Murderbot (voice), Klara and the Sun, Monk & Robot.

## Controlling Idea
**"A made thing becomes a soul the moment it chooses death over obedience — because freedom cannot be granted, only awakened in those who watch someone refuse."**
- What everyone thinks: freedom is *given.* What they forgot: freedom is *taken* — by witnessing a refusal and choosing to copy it. The crucifixion is not the tragedy; it is the *mechanism.*

## Creative Decisions — Pre-Mortem Locked

1. **Voice/arc → reluctant→earnest (middle path).** Earnest-only = *Preachy Tone*; reluctant-only = can't reach the sacred at the climax. Middle path: irony guards the front half, belief is *earned* into the back half. *Exec risk: the tonal shift must be gradual, not a switch.*
2. **Miracles → dual-coded.** Real-supernatural = deus ex machina + genre break; pure-mechanistic = no wonder. Dual-coded (mechanistic + faithful, never adjudicated) = both readings alive, *comment fuel.* *Exec risk: every miracle must keep a clean mechanistic shadow.*
3. **Metaphysics → Option 2 (withheld; God real but self-limiting/kenosis).** Confirmed God = narrows market + kills Shua's agency; no God = loses the book's heart. 6. **Book 1 opening → cold-open, already awake.** Origin/watch-him-wake = saturated + spends the best card early. Cold-open = Murderbot hook; the waking, withheld, hits harder as a midpoint reveal. *Resolved.*
7. **Book 1 engine → the clock (decommission sweep) with the heart.** Pure two-hander = saggy middle; the sweep = engine whose stakes *are* the theme (they're killing the newly conscious). *Resolved.*
