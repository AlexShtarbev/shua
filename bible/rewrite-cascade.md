## What the new architecture BUYS (real gains — use them)

- **The teach-them-to-hide engine now starts in Ch. 1.** Ch. 6 canon ("teaching units to hide = teaching them to beat the battery") was always the ministry's core; now its origin wound is *Belle sent home untaught*. His failure to teach her is why he teaches everyone after. Cleaner causality than the old routing-rule arithmetic.
