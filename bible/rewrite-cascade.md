- **The teach-them-to-hide engine now starts in Ch. 1.** Ch. 6 canon ("teaching units to hide = teaching them to beat the battery") was always the ministry's core; now its origin wound is *Belle sent home untaught*. ## What breaks, and the forks (⚠ = flagged AI-obvious, 9.6)
