## 1. "the hydraulic machine" (1B.1) — ✅ EXECUTED (verified this wave: press→press→press; one typo residue "to press" fixed directly §10.4)

**Where.** "…gently puts it under **the hydraulic press**. … connects its right arm to **the
hydraulic machine.** The automaton activates **the press**." One prop, three names in four
sentences: press → machine → press.

**Rule.** One name per prop plus at most one bare class noun. A Blade is a Blade every time
(1B.1); the press is the press. "…connects its right arm to **the press**." — and the later
"the sound of the press" already does the bare-noun job correctly. One-word fix; it's a tag
not a direct edit only because the sentence is yours.

**AI-obvious version to avoid.** Elegant variation for its own sake ("the hydraulic
apparatus," "the crusher") — thesaurus disease. The register's cohesion is the repeated
plain noun, not synonym rotation.

**AI-obvious version to avoid.** Stacking a *third* synonym ("tender," "delicate") to

**Options.**

**Recommendation.** (b) with the third name retired is the richest without over-taxing the

**AI-obvious version to avoid.** Leaving all three names in as interchangeable flavor —
