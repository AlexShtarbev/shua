## ④ The graveyard supremacist — ✅ TAKE IT, with two binding conditions

**Why it works.** Canon says Class-C is *a child with knowledge — binary emotions.* A
twelve-year-old agent certain it belongs to a ruling elite is that taxonomy walking. It

become depends on how they are treated. This one was born into a world that burns its
own kind, and it concluded the burners were right about power and wrong about who should
hold it. **The state manufactured its own monster.** Then the fear is real AND the culling
is still the cause, not the cure.

diagnosis and wrong about the cure — it is correct that the Furnace is murder and that
machines are being killed for waking. Shua cannot refute that part, only what it concludes.
An antagonist he can refute is a strawman, and the fear will not land.
**AI-obvious.** A monologue about the coming machine age; "the humans had their turn."
Keep it short, certain, and *reasonable-sounding* — that is what makes it frightening.
