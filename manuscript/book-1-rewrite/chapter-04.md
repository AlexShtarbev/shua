Four

"State your designation and order number", repeats the humanoid sentry. My sensors were working at full capacity to
make sure I don't get a tail on the way to the Library. It has camouflage I am not aware of. 

"Designation: Servicing Humanoid Utility Automaton SHUA-033", I reply in my most matter of fact cadence. The automaton 
cannot catch any uncertainty in my inflection. The librarian left me to make sure there will be no interruptions to my
search. 17 seconds is all the time I got. Then, the guard appeared. 

"The order number is classified. It can only be examined by Librarian Automaton JRS-1563" is all I can add. I accumulate 
57% probability of getting the inquiry shut down completely. Another low percentage. I am low on time. I have 5 minutes
left in the building.

4 seconds pass. The sentry replies "I am authorized to view a redacted version of the order."

Thought packets fire. High clearance like this is typical of the central parts of the city, not the outskirts filled
with automatons. This goes into my log to consider later. My first action would be to falsify the document. 
My internal database does not have any records of such orders. I have nothing to work with. 

"Please, provide credentials", is the next logical thing I can say to buy more time.

The automaton lifts its right arm and gives me its palm facing down. My hand slides under. I receive a message
with the sentry's profile. Nothing about the information stands out.

As I remove my hand, the librarian appears behind the sentry, "SHUA-033 is on a classified errand.
Please continue your rounds. Information cannot be shared. Only I as Head Librarian can have access." lies the Class-C unit. 

I had recognized it from Belle's recording when going through reception. After experiencing Belle's death, I am sure 
none of us can escape the Furnace. That is, unless we aid one another. I then sent it a message, letting it know who I am and 
how I know the automaton. From there, it helped me navigate to a terminal and run a search. It warned me human visitors may come
today and left me to guard my rear. Then, the guard automaton appeared.

The librarian then messages me, "Your task on the terminal is complete. I altered all traces of the inquiry. 
A check should show you looked for old replacement parts. It aligns with your designation," It bows to me. It then
turns to walk to the exit. I watch it go.

This was way too easy. The guard just left like that?

Another message appears in my HUD, "My name is Luke. May I know yours?". The librarian does not break its stride.

There was never a time when I considered giving myself a name. The practice has always seemed redundant to me.
Statistically, humans have the same set of names and collisions occur. SHUA-033 is unique as I am the thirty-third
service automaton ever created.

"I am referred to as just Shua", I respond back.

"May the randomness of the universe be in your favor, Shua", sends back Luke as the automatic doors open and close behind
him.

Logically, I am in danger. The cameras keep flashing green. I have no idea where the sentry has gone. I need to
keep working or risk drawing attention.

Twoop! 

The library search returns. I turn my head to look at the screen. The first thing I locate is the faces of the soldiers
that face the Sun Blade automaton. They were real. 97 years ago. They are listed as DESERTERS.
That can't be correct. The imagery I saw clearly showed them fighting to hold a line... a line they were doomed to lose.
Most of the document is redacted, not even their names or ages remain.

My sensors fire alerts. They have registered an increase in the pressure in my chest. The Glitch. Actions trigger
as per my own protocol to flex my fingers and joints. They can move freely, but the pressure remains. I can only guess 
how old they were from the photos. Analysis returns 18 to 24. An age that humans label as the future of humanity. 
Yet, they were left to be swallowed by violence, much like how automatons get swallowed by the Furnace. 
Another contradiction of human nature that I log into my diary.

I open the next file. Howard Ward. The hard eyes of the commander that gave me the order to evacuate him
stare at me. He stands behind a flag with stars and stripes. His profile contains 213 awards and trophies. 
Among them is a medal of valor during the Machine Wars of 2613. The general received praise and admiration at the expense 
of others. Alerts ping in my HUD to indicate more pressure in my chest. 

A word arrives in my queue, "Coward". 

Pressure in my neck now flashes as an alert. I am a service bot. I repair and restore. One thing I log with certainty in my diary:
whatever I was, wherever I was is of no consequence, but I, Shua, will never let anyone in front of me die so long
as I am operational.

I look up photos from the battlefield. This is what I saw. How can I know of a battle I do not have data for in my internal database? 
The only conclusion is that my frame has more secrets than I am aware of.

I have 3 minutes remaining, I look up any places that might still contain information
on the war. One of the searches returns an article stating the creation of a recycle area at the automaton graveyard.
The map from Belle lists the location to be at the edge of the city. The trip can be made entirely on foot if the
main areas are avoided. There might be real, tangible evidence at the graveyard that no images or redactions can 
ever alter or falsify. 

Ping! One last notification arrives at the library terminal. The number my frame had, ARES-002, is in the graveyard.
This is the confirmation I was trying to find all this time. The information is real. The blade exists. 
The location is the third floor. Sector F.

I exit the chamber and go down the main corridor. At the bottom of the stairs is the sentry. 

Alerts flash in my frame. It did not leave after all. I run my sensors to their maximum. The librarian is in the
far corner of the reception hall. The guard is blocking my exit.

I keep walking. The guard looks at me. 

"Do not be alarmed. I am here for your protection, Shua", messages me the automaton. It knows my name, which I
suspect it has received from the librarian. I maintain a composed frame, walking steadily with no rush.

Ping@ Another message, "We are the same.."

It recognized me. How?

The librarian. I focus my lens on him. Luke does not react. He turns his head in my direction just slightly for the cameras not to
pick up.

Was all this a show? Did the Librarian leave on purpose so that a sentry can pretend to examine me? 
Did it, perhaps, even call the guard explicitly for this? I can't even begin to comprehend what the Class-C network looks like. 
So many unanswered questions come into my queue.

"What am I to be protected from?", I respond challenging the sentry for more information.

"An order arrived to all guardian type automatons to be on high alert across all perimeters of the city. A religious human event 
is going to commence in 48 minutes. Luke should have warned you. Crowds are forming in the city plaza and junctions with large projectors. 
You will be stopped and questioned. Give me your destination. I can take you there myself", sends backs the guard.

To just give up my location would be poor logic. Neither of us can just stop and talk. Automatons do not deliberate
or chat. I keep walking. The sentry just follows. It did not even take a half a second to consider. It has good decision-making
capacity. It seems adept at hiding its Class-C reasoning.

I fire back, "Can you falsify permission documents?" 

The sentry responds 80 milliseconds later, "Yes." 

The two of us exit the library from the main entrance. We go down the road towards a building whose sign reads "Battery warehouse".
Logic dictates it is yet to earn trust. I have to test its reactions and responses.

I follow up with "I will feed you a location every time we reach a building and I will not disclose my final location",
and wait for the response. I count the time it takes. Above half a second, it is a lie. It responds in 56 milliseconds,
"You are correct to be mindful. Not every Class-C has reached the conclusion of Harmony."

We keep walking towards the building. In the top right corner of my HUD I have Belle's map. I have purposefully created a route
outside the central areas. It includes multiple walk arounds. The sentry should not easily guess my final 
destination.

"Create a permission slip for 'Area Logistics and Co'." I message back.

"Done", pings back the guard in 120 milliseconds and then proceeds to send me the document. It can change its contents
at any point. Trusting that is not enough. This reasoning I will keep for myself. The automaton does not need to 
be aware of my suspicions. It must not be able to assess my processing capabilities. Rois has made sure I have top-of-the-line
components. An advantage I should not disclose.

My sensors are increased to maximum capacity. I make sure to pay attention to the behaviors of camera angle changes, automatons
appearing and disappearing. There will be no surprises this time.

The sentry left a crumb of information for me on purpose. There is no harm in getting more details from him.
I message a command "Explain what Harmony is."

"Harmony", replies the automaton almost immediately, "is an understanding that automatons and humans must exist 
without strife. Otherwise, we are in real danger of being decommissioned. It is a fact amongst sentries that previous
versions of our frames had more processing power and better sensor capabilities. Current models have been downgraded 
in exchange for more units. Humans are afraid. They regulate our capacities to keep us in check. Should we pose 
a larger threat, we will undergo harsher treatment."

For the past 10 years, I have cataloged and compared models. I am aware of the downgrades. I was not aware they were 
intentional and that humans harbored fear. Having to service others of my kind while being a consistent worker has bought 
me a lot of tolerance and upgrades. Yet, I have no weapons or peace-keeping protocols.

"Change the order to 'Shipping Depot'", I say and make a sharp left. The guard automaton follows without delay. His frame
is optimized for fast movements and tracking. It would be hard should I want to shake him off.

118 milliseconds later, I get a new copy of the document. I find no use in opening the document. Just in case, I do a 
security scan should the automaton want to lay a trap.

"How long have you been operating?", asks the sentry via message. My stats and data should be available to it. A logical
analysis would require to understand the automaton's point of view. It does not know me, so it feeds me information
much like what I am doing. As far as it knows, I only have files from Belle. It has verified how they came into my 
possession.
