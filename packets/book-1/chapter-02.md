## 4. Craft cues (watch-list first)

biggest bait: the why-now of the fire, the what-do-I-do at the dock, the frame line.
Rule of thumb this chapter: if a question's next sentence doesn't widen it (1B.9), it's
deflection-filler — cut it and let the paragraph assert. The frame line counts against

**② Opener frames (1B.7).** Present tense wants to open every paragraph "I + verb."
Watch specifically for runs of "I have / I am." Openers to reach for instead: the object
