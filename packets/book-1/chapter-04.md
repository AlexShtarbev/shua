## 4. Craft cues (watch-list first)

**① 2.6 (ceiling):** her register converts questions into tested statements. If a
paragraph wants "Who profited?", let it — that's her ONE kind of question (cui bono);
everything else asserts.

**② 1B.7:** watch "She + verb" runs. Items, objects, and verdicts can take the opener
slot. The file, not the woman, can be the subject for whole stretches.

**③ 0-frag:** her fragments are labels and verdicts ("Item three." "CLEAR." "First.").
Beat-marking still applies — a fragment is her gavel coming down.

**④ Operator grammar (§2C):** retro-brief her moves (the telemetry already pulled before
we hear she pulled it); ledger everything (times, counts, minutes — exact numbers for
the world, 2B.9-inverse: she can afford precision everywhere EXCEPT her father, where
drops stay unnumbered); pain gets a participle, if it appears at all.

**⑤ The irony engine (2.2/2.8/6.12):** never wink. Her reads are BETTER reasoning than
the truth deserves — that's what makes the wrongness tragic. Reread test: every item
should be crueler the second time, because the reader watched him do it.

**⑥ Voice-gap enforcement (2.12/3.2):** Ward speaks in deliverables and ends first; she
speaks in measurements. Neither touches Shua's register. If a dry line could get a laugh,
it's his, not hers — recut.

**⑦ Loose threads (9.4):** the carrier/band is NOT in this chapter (she finds it Ch. 6);
the door she closed quietly; the bounty nobody has claimed; who profits (nobody — leave
the hole taut).
