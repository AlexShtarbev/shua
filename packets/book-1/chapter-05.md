## 2. Beat sheet

   lies is a question with no answer in his files. *Deposit:* not-knowing as the new

### Movement II — Cairn on the line

   3.15 native to a robot narrator. The catalog is warm — cap it before it gets cute
   (one note gets glossed wrong-on-purpose is plenty of wit).

### Movement III — The porter (9:12)
6. **Intake: the hammering arm.** Four beats a second; crew backs off; cutout not
   answering; the third shout is always the press lane now. *Establishes:* stakes local
   and immediate; the house crest (loved, polished, shipped with a waiver — the
   politeness-maxim family in object form).
7. **He is already walking.** The accuracy-about-the-walking beat: no stride, nothing
   shone — a technician crossing a dock toward a flagged intake. *Establishes:* the
   anti-legend register; also the retrospective shadow ("what gets said about it" —
   fold into the frame spend or cut; budget is one).
8. **The diagnosis: a trauma loop.** A stuck loop replays; it doesn't rage. A fall caught
   mid-write, days old by the wear. *Requires:* readable in the rhythm — his eye works
   before his hands; the reader learns the fault BEFORE the fix (6.18: taught →
   demonstrated).
9. **The fix, in craft grammar.** Time the loop, catch the wrist, pin it, port,
   clock-starve, split, flush, reseat — with **"Easy. I have you."** inside it (anchor).
   *Establishes:* THE first miracle = a repair, wholly. Domain-word site #1.
10. **The Glitch along the work** — keeping time with the hands, not pointing. He has no
    procedure for what that means; the usual file. *Establishes:* glitch grammar
    escalation; dual-coded, unadjudicated.
11. **The frame line** — the on-the-record designation of the first one (a stuck loop in
    a porter's buffer; those exact words). One spend; nothing clever after it.

### Movement IV — The stillness
12. **The dock after: every optic turned, and staying turned.** Movement over; tracking
    without a mover. Machines looking at stillness. *Establishes:* the congregation
    forming — shown by firmware behavior, never named (9.2).
13. **Cairn across the floor, load half-lifted.** Seen by machines only. *Establishes:*

    two kinds of being seen; the wrong one is watching).
14. **The shift runs ordinary, if you trust the cameras.** The fixable column + the other
    arithmetic (one porter, forty optics, unknown buffers writing the morning down).
    *Deposit:* columns want-word; the count of witnesses as a number he can't file.

### Movement V — The sweeper (6:40)
15. **The link chimes: a sweeper.** Invisible-by-familiarity; parked at his bench.
    *Establishes:* the least of these as first witness-by-choice.
16. **The clip: forty seconds, off every grid.** Unlogged-links canon walked plainly
    (1C.10): links don't route, nobody logs, nothing a machine would say to a machine —
    land on what that means: the one road the cameras don't own. *Establishes:* THE ROAD
    OF THE GOSPEL.
17. **Why him? He doesn't know.** One unanswered beat (counts against question budget;
    earns its place). A machine returns what it finds to its owner — maybe the moment
    was his. Leave taut.
18. **He watches it twice, body between screen and camera** — an angle he knows without

19. **Curtain: the problem, named.** On the clip the repair doesn't look like a repair —
    **it looks like an answer.** Hard stop (bridge to Ch. 6). Past-tense curtain ¶
