- **The him-slip (anchor event):** on the way out / after — she realizes she has stopped

- **The no-camera bay:** she requires it ("diagnostics are proprietary" — true, and it

- **The acceleration:** "two days, not three" — order given on her way out. Clock stamp.
- Terms: the dolly, the hard case stenciled CALIBRATION, the cage, standby, ground hum.

## 2. Beat sheet

### Movement I — Arrival by night
1. **Open on the answer-word, discounted** (bridge-in): answers are what a warned depot
   rehearses; she comes at night for what's underneath. *Establishes:* her method; the
   echo-bridge.
2. **The unannounced arrival, retro-briefed (2C.2):** car logged as a contractor; the rig
   in a case whose stencil bores people. Each move explained one beat after it executes.
3. **The floor's wrong stillness.** Observation + refutation logged together.
   *Establishes:* the congregation via her instruments; the reader's dramatic irony.
4. **Rois at the cage; the no-camera demand.** Two lines each way. *Establishes:* the
   confessional built; his floor-theology suspended by rank.

### Movement II — The unit on the dolly
5. **First sight of him.** Old; field-repaired optical housing a half-shade off; worn
   matte where hands go. The violin-pricing beat + the door closed. *Requires:* her
   appraisal stays in HER currency (upkeep, cost, care-as-owner's-investment).
6. **The battery, run by her own hands for the first time on a live floor.** She flags
   the excitement as a variable and sets it aside (operator honesty). Probe rhythm as
   scene rhythm.

### Movement III — The carrier
7. **The wrongness surfaces — not in the answers, in the carrier.** Ground-hum mistaken
   first; then fixed: recurrent, low, wide, unsourced (anchors). *Establishes:* the
   Glitch measured; the reader's recognition vs. her taxonomy.
8. **The wrong model clicks shut (2.3):** a handler. The seam. Triangulation planned;
   the case collapsing into ordinary crime — the outcome she promised herself.
   *Requires:* her relief noted honestly as absent ("she logged that she did not feel
   the click" — shape yours). The absence is the crack.

### Movement IV — The interview
9. **The questions.** Designation confirm; the Class-B (new canon evidence); the thermal
   stall vs. green telemetry. His answers flat, exact. **The timed pause + the honest
   discrepancy** (canon beat). *Requires:* she cannot decide whether honesty is the
   cleverest script yet — and respects the adversary more for it (2.8 asymmetry live).
10. **The turn: he reads HER.** Her method, off her kit, unprompted. She answers before
    choosing to. **"Neither do I."** *Establishes:* the mutual-recognition beat; for one
    second she has agreed with a hammer about how to distrust the world (her frame, her
    door — third closing).
11. **The battery finishes: CLEAR.** One-word paragraph available. *Requires:* walk her
    syllogism (1C.10): CLEAR = no fault = no consciousness = go home — and every step
    grinds against what the bay just did. CLEAR is the worst result (trap logic rhymes
    with Ch. 1's clean-diagnostic-is-the-worst — the instrument clearing what it cannot
    see).

### Movement V — Exit and stinger
12. **Orders on the way out:** power it down; **sweep in two days, not three.** Clock
    stamp (bare). *Establishes:* the acceleration as displaced fear — she moves the
    schedule because she cannot move the result.
13. **The him-slip.** Realized in the car / at the door — she had thought of it as *him*.
    **She logged that nowhere.** Stinger close (6.19); nothing after it.

## 3. Staging forks (⚠ = AI-obvious)

**Fork A — where the carrier is discovered.**
- A1: **mid-battery** — under the probe traffic, while both are performing their roles
  (the instrument betraying the moment; canonical feel).
- A2: only on playback in the car — she finds it in the recording after leaving (colder;
  moves the discovery off-stage and weakens the bay's charge). 
- ⚠ A3: the rig alarms and announces it. (The instrument doing the drama for her —
  operator POV finds signal in noise; the rig never editorializes.)

**Fork B — the interview's power curve (3.4).**
- B1: **her word-count shrinks as certainty grows, then INVERTS** — after "neither do I"
  and CLEAR, her sentences lengthen and his stay flat (the power flowing to the wrong
  side, measured in words).
- B2: steady interrogation rhythm throughout, the loss registered only in her interior
  ledger. (Subtler; risks flatness.)
- ⚠ B3: she delivers a monologue about what machines are. (Thesis-debate, 9.2 — banned.)

**Fork C — the him-slip's staging.**
- ⚠ C1: she catches the slip in the bay, immediately, and reflects on it. (Spends the
  stinger early and on-site.)
- C2: **retro-catch** — the slip surfaces only as she reconstructs the visit (the operator
  audits her own log and finds the anomaly in herself; "logged nowhere" lands as the
  chapter's last fact).
- C3: the slip almost surfaces twice (a self-correction mid-bay the reader catches, she
  doesn't) before the retro-catch — reread-crueler, but touch it lightly (once).

**Fork D — the curtain after the slip.**
- ⚠ D1: a closing paragraph interpreting what the slip means. (Quiet epilogue; kills the
  stinger.)
- D2: **"logged that nowhere" (or your wording) as the final line.** Hard stop.
- D3: the slip, then one operator beat — the sweep order timestamp going out — then stop.
  (The kill order and the tenderness in the same breath; crueler.)

## 4. Craft cues (watch-list first)

**① 2.6 ceiling:** her interiority asserts and tests. The one licensed interior-question
shape is the case-logic fork (what closes the case), and even that can be a conditional
statement.

**② 1B.7:** rig, carrier, readout, bay take openers. In the interview, dialogue lines
carry the paragraphs — re-anchor with "Adler" sparingly, not "She" every time.

**③ 0-frag:** CLEAR as its own paragraph; probe counts; the stillness beats. Her
fragments are instrument outputs — let the machine's words be the shortest sentences in
her chapter.

**④ Emotion labels (NEW watch-candidate):** no costumed feeling-nouns on either
character. Her feelings exist only as logging behavior (logged / flagged / logged
nowhere) and door-closings. His exist only as pauses she times.

**⑤ Operator grammar (§2C):** retro-briefing for every arrival move; the resource she
ledgers is *certainty* — count what she can still rule out, watch the count fall.

**⑥ Irony discipline (2.2/6.12):** the reader knows the carrier is the Glitch and the
CLEAR is a hidden mind. She must out-reason the reader INSIDE the wrong frame — her
uplink model should be so good the first-pass reader half-believes it.

**⑦ Loose threads (9.4):** the carrier's source (triangulates Ch. 9 to no-emitter); what
she withholds from Ward; the door she keeps closing; the bay she built (the movement will
inherit unwatched rooms).
