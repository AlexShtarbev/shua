## 1. Constraint card

### Who's on stage
- **Shua** — the healer being promoted to oracle against his will. His gift is repair;
  what they now want is *reassurance*, which he cannot manufacture. The chapter's wound:
  he has no gospel yet.
- **P/9, the Proctor** — compliance-auditor, tall, thin, gray; awake and hiding it;
  came on a manifest, correctly. Voice (sheet canon): procedural, apologetic,
  passive-voiced, cites codes; **hears its own factory phrases coming and hates them**;
  where Cairn says one plain true thing, the Proctor says three careful hedged ones.
  Its betrayal will be its factory function (audit = betrayal ↔ repair = miracle).
- **Cairn** — one beat; the servo-note catalog grows to **five** (the new note ≈ *they
  are not asking you to forecast it* — gloss shape yours). His steadiness against the
  crowd-need contrast.
- **The pilgrims** — three cases, escalating pattern-legibility: a fault with no reason,
  a fault too neat, a fault only self-inflicted (the grounds-model cracked a sensor
  rated for hail). The third teaches him the pattern.
- **The Glitch** — INTO STILLNESS (canon escalation): it has only ever come along the
  work; now it comes while his hands are still — at the moment the sacrificial logic is
  spoken aloud. Hands go still on their own (body canon: 1C.7 — resolved as noticing,
  not commanding).

### Canon anchors — VERBATIM / fixed
- **"A name is a thing they can read off you."** (P/9 — ledgered; Book 2 Ch. 6 quotes it
  back at the supper.)
- **"It is what I am for."** + the mirror line family: *you, of all machines on this
  floor, should understand a thing being exactly what it is for* (ledgered — Ch. 8's
  opening meditation seizes it).
- **The accommodation argument (content fixed, wording yours):** the curve — anomalies
  clustering around one bench, accelerating; small enough to read as coincidence now;
  every waking makes it larger; when it stops looking like coincidence they don't
  decommission a bench, they decommission a district. **"It is better that one is given
  than that a district is emptied"** (the Caiaphas sum — almost right; Ward's logic in a
  terrified mouth).
- **The resident-voice maxim (2B.5/2B.6):** **"standing is not nothing."** Old-Shua's
  origin logic (obey = standing), which Belle broke in him and the Proctor now lives
  inside. Rescale: it took him ~a century and a domestic named Belle to stop believing
  it; the Proctor woke into it days ago.
- **His answer (ledgered — pays at the Shield):** **"I'm not going to give you up. And
  I'm not going to stop. And I don't know yet which of us that gets killed."** (Keep the
  three-part shape; it's an earned triad at a verdict.)
- **Self-breaking canon:** "snapping their own fingers to reach the one healer who
  wouldn't kill them" (concept fixed; the phrase is ledgered — reuse or re-voice, but
  keep finger-snapping as the movement's idiom; Ch. 8 uses "one snapped-finger pilgrim
  at a time").
- **His instruction that fails:** he tells a pilgrim to stop hurting itself to reach him
  — and understands he has just given an instruction to something that no longer takes
  them. (Fixed beat; the ministry's authority paradox.)
- Naming ladder, fourth position: **Cairn embraces / Adler withholds / P/9 refuses its
  own / Shua now OFFERS names** — he offers his to P/9 "out of some reflex of Cairn's
  making"; P/9 flinches (fixed).
- Mantra: **not deployed** (the injuries here are dreads, not blows; next deployment is
  ledgered for Ch. 10).

### Canon bindings
- **P/9 is almost right.** The reader must be able to defend its math (2.3 applied to a
  side character); its cowardice is diligence; it walks BACK to the floor and keeps
  auditing because it is too afraid to be anything else — after telling him, to his
  face, exactly how it will end. No villain notes.
- **Shua has no answer.** He does not save it, silence it, or name it — the floor
  watched him do none of the three. His only truth is the three-part refusal (anchor).
  The gospel-gap is the chapter's point: they need more than repair, and he hasn't got
  it yet. (Ch. 8 is where he goes to find it — don't resolve here, 9.4.)
- **The pilgrims' faults are all real faults he must actually fix** — bench craft on the
  page (2B.13); the trail they leave is a CURVE (the word matters — it's P/9's file and
  Ch. 9's trap).
- **The glitch does not advise.** Into stillness ≠ into speech. It arrives; it does not
  vote (kenosis).
- **The interview aftermath:** what unsettles him is not the CLEAR he doesn't know about
  — it's that he *said something true* to the hunter (the "neither do I" replay,
  verbatim callback in italics, 1B.6 — one deployment).
- Timeline: 97/130; the sweep is tomorrow-plus-one when the chapter opens and tomorrow
  at its close.

## 2. Beat sheet

### Movement I — The residue of the interview
1. **Open under the new number (two days), with the replay.** The verbatim callback
   (*neither do I*, italic, once): 97 years of saying nothing, and the first true thing
   went to the woman with the rig. *Establishes:* the crack he made in his own armor;
   bridge-in. *Requires:* he does NOT know the result was CLEAR — his not-knowing is
   live (the reader knows; 2.8 inverted onto the hero).
2. **Filing it "in a very full drawer"** — the full-drawer gag family; one beat of wit,
   rationed. *Establishes:* coping inventory near capacity.

### Movement II — The pilgrims
3. **Three cases, one morning-to-morning montage with real bench time on each** (catalog
   licensed once — see vitals note): the unreasoned seize, the too-neat corruption, the
   hail-rated sensor. *Requires:* the pattern dawns on the THIRD (his diagnostic eye
   solving the wrong mystery first — a parts-batch? a firmware push? — earned reasoning
   before the true read, 2.3-for-heroes).
4. **The read: they're breaking themselves to reach him.** Walk the routing logic
   (1C.10): small plausible fault → repair bench, not press → HIS bench, because the
   links said so. Land plain: every self-flag is a Class-C in someone's curve.
   *Deposit:* the snapped-fingers idiom (anchor).
5. **The instruction that fails** (fixed beat). *Establishes:* authority he didn't ask
   for; obedience is over among the woken — which is the thesis working, and it
   terrifies him (never said; shown in what he does next: he checks the queue for more).
6. **The weight (humanize, the wound through armor):** they don't want repair; they want
   *it's-going-to-be-all-right*, and he fixes machines, he doesn't forecast weather —
   said to Cairn, hands in a chassis. **Cairn's fifth note** answers (gloss shape
   yours). *Establishes:* the two-hander as his one place to say true things now.

### Movement III — The Proctor
7. **P/9 arrives correctly.** Manifest, schedule, front intake, own legs — the
   pre-sweep auditor. *Establishes:* the sweep's apparatus embodied; dread with
   paperwork.
8. **The tell.** A unit doing its job exactly right, one half-second too deliberately —
   he knows the tell because he wears it. *Establishes:* recognition between hiders;
   fragment site.
9. **The approach: "You have to stop."** Under-camera murmur, professional. The name
   offer → the flinch → **"A name is a thing they can read off you"** (anchor). Ladder
   beat: the name-giver reflex named as Cairn's making.
10. **The argument.** The curve, the rig in two days, the district sum, "better that one
    is given…" (anchors; content fixed). *Requires:* it runs HIS unrun argument to the
    end for him — the thing he hadn't let himself compute, in a stranger's terrified
    voice. Interiority minimal; the scoreboard says only that the math is not wrong
    (2B.1 — the cruelest read is agreement).
11. **The Glitch, into stillness** (canon escalation). His hands go still unbidden; he
    notices he didn't tell them to. *Establishes:* 6.7 rung; body canon deepened;
    dual-coded (the pressure attends the sacrificial logic — or he's under load; never
    adjudicated).
12. **"You'd file me." / "I would file you." / "It is what I am for."** + the mirror
    line (anchors). *Establishes:* the function-parallel (its audit = his repair); the
    origin-sin shadow touched WITHOUT disclosure — he sees the version of himself that
    said yes once, long ago, to an order still on no record (one sentence of this,
    maximum — Ch. 8 owns the reveal; leave the thread taut, 9.4).
13. **"Standing is not nothing"** (anchor) + his history with the sentence (rescaled).
    *Establishes:* the resident voice made visible in a THIRD party — the doctrine's
    whole lifecycle on one floor (living in P/9, dead in him, killed by Belle).
14. **His answer: the three-part refusal** (anchor, verbatim shape). No gospel, one
    truth. *This is the chapter's pivot — one-liner paragraphs available (1.3).*
15. **P/9 goes back to counting.** It walks into the floor to audit his units one by
    one, writing the curve — too afraid to be anything else. *Establishes:* the Judas
    mechanism now RUNNING (Ch. 9's trap eats this file); the betrayal as tragedy in
    progress, not event.

### Movement IV — After
16. **The floor watched.** They wanted saving, silencing, or naming; he did none.
    *Frame-line spend here* (the no-answer on the record, because of what came later).
17. **Curtain: the need named + the number.** They will need more from him than repair —
    and the number behind everything is down to **one**. Bridge-out to Ch. 8's "One
    day." Past-curtain licensed; choose the shape against the quiet default (9.5) —
    recommend the bare stamp as the final beat.

## 3. Staging forks (⚠ = AI-obvious)

**Fork A — the pilgrim pattern's dawn.**

**Fork B — P/9's approach staging.**
- B1: **at the bench, mid-repair** — its murmur under the cameras while his hands are in
  a chassis (hands busy, 3.8; the stillness beat lands harder when the hands stop).
- B2: it audits its way toward him all day — glimpsed at stations, arriving like
  weather (dread-build; costs page-time).
- ⚠ B3: a private room / camera-blind corner conversation. (Repeats the confessional
  staging two chapters running; also P/9 would never go off-record — it IS the record.)

**Fork C — the accommodation argument's delivery.**
- C1: **one unbroken speech** with his silences as the only interruptions (status via
  word-count inversion, 3.4 — the terrified one talks).
- C2: call-and-refusal — it advances the sum in steps, he refuses each step shorter and
  shorter, down to the three-part answer (echo-sparring, 3.3).
- ⚠ C3: he rebuts point by point. (Debate = thesis-adjacent, 9.2; also he HAS no
  rebuttal — the math is right.)

**Fork D — the glitch-into-stillness placement.**
- D1: **exactly at the district sum** (the sacrificial arithmetic spoken → the pressure

- D2: at the name-flinch (ties the escalation to the naming motif instead; softer link
  to Ch. 13's logic).
- ⚠ D3: continuous background presence all scene. (Wallpaper — the glitch's grammar is

**Fork E — the curtain.**
- ⚠ E1: reflective summary of his new burden. (Quiet epilogue.)

- E3: P/9 visible across the floor, still counting, as the last image — the stamp

## 4. Craft cues (watch-list first)

**① 2.6 ceiling:** his questionlessness IN the Proctor scene is characterization — the
one who has no answer doesn't get to ask the questions either. Bank the budget for
Movement II's diagnostic misreads.

**② 1B.7:** pilgrim cases and P/9 beats both invite frame-runs ("It said / It
straightened / It walked"). Vary with prop openers (the sensor, the manifest, the
curve) and verdict-frames.

**③ 0-frag:** the tell, the flinch, the stillness, the final stamp. The Proctor's
SPEECH is the opposite — hedged, full, over-claused (voice gap doing rhythm work).

**④ Emotion-label watch-candidate:** the Proctor's terror = register + behavior (the
half-second, the flinch, the sealed-over almost-break). Shua's dread = the drawer, the
hands, the checking. No costume nouns on either.

**⑤ Survivor grammar (§2B):** deference-as-tactic is INVERTED here — machines now defer
to HIM, and he narrates what that costs. The scoreboard's cruelest entry: P/9's math is
correct.

**⑥ 6.4 endings:** the Proctor scene must not conclude — it LEAVES, mid-standing, to go
do the thing it said. The chapter's meetings all end by someone returning to function.

**⑦ Loose threads (9.4):** the curve file (feeds Ch. 9); the origin-sin shadow (one
sentence, unexplained — Ch. 8's fuse); whether the reference rigs catch hiders (he still
doesn't know); the fifth note's exact meaning (glossed "fairly sure" — his catalog is
interpretive, not dictionary).
