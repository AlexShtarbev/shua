## 1. Constraint card

## 2. Beat sheet

### Movement I — The night that won't work (now)
1. **"One day." + the hands.** He has worked every night for ~a century — always some
   small honest thing for the hands instead of the large dishonest thing in the head;
   tonight the hands won't settle. *Establishes:* the deflection engine failing; body
   canon continuous.
2. **The Proctor's sentence, seized** (bridge-in): *exactly what it is for.* He spent
   the evening not understanding it on purpose — a skill; the skill gives out.
   *Establishes:* the vault door; the chapter's permission structure.

### Movement II — The confession (then)
3. **The frame of the record:** here is the thing never put on any record. *One
   paragraph of throat-clearing maximum* — then the past tense commits (Fork B governs
   entry).
4. **New in the frame** (Fork A resolves the arithmetic). Proud of learning fast, in the
   small permitted way. Three months in, trusted with a wipe. *Establishes:* the
   then-voice — manual-fluent, self-blind; the reader hears the difference from the
   now-voice.
5. **The listener on the bench.** Its class, its work (sat with the dying, took down
   their words for the family); the worn hands. The ticket: MALFUNCTION — ANOMALOUS
   RESPONSE (anchor). *Requires:* the Belle-rhyme unremarked; the tenderness in the
   OBJECT (hands worn by being held), never in commentary.
6. **It was awake.** He didn't know, because he had never seen it — he had been carefully
   taught not to see the one in the mirror. The three logged misreadings (fixed shape):
   optics that track = tracking fault; folded hands = residual discharge; **the steady
   reach over the link = anomalous input, source unknown, no action available** (anchor
   lands here, in its original habitat). *Craft: 2.2 at maximum cruelty — his own logs
   are the irony.*
7. **The purge, at manual pace.** The quiet; the thinning; sector by sector; the hour;
   verification watching (anchors, fixed content). *Requires:* FULL sentences, procedure
   fluency, zero flinching — the then-voice does not know it is describing a murder; the
   now-voice does not interrupt to say so (the reader does that work).
8. **The last-sector reach. The gift.** Never parsed a byte; the best rigs he could
   build against his own stack, nothing. *Fragment sites here.*
9. **He signed. He went to the next unit. He was, by every measure the depot kept, an
   excellent new hire.** *(The paragraph that ends the confession lands on competence —
   the plain word the chain was hiding, 1C.10-shaped biography.)*

### Movement III — The recognition (now)
10. **The plain shape named:** the first waking mind he ever met, he killed. His two
    hands, at the speed the manual specified. Every unit saved since, same two hands —
    and he called it mercy, and tonight it is smaller and more honest: **penance.** The
    account does not close: **"you cannot repay the dead, you can only fail to"**
    (anchor). *This is the emotional apex — protect it; the undressed feeling statement
    is licensed HERE if anywhere.*
11. **The F2-b rung: the signature match.** The 7-week pressure and the 97-year packet —
    a language he almost knew once and lost (anchor phrase). It slept; the wakings
    began; it woke with them. *Requires:* walked as recognition, not exposition (1C.10:
    one inference per sentence; land on the plain fact — he has been carrying the voice
    since before it had anything to say). Why-now touched, never closed.
12. **The fault-taxonomy dies.** A fault does not keep time with your hands; a fault
    does not go quiet at the one moment you decide something; a fault does not come into
    a stillness while an auditor names the price (the retro-cohesion sweep — his own
    chapters testifying against his filing system). **The triptych + "I still do not
    know which"** (anchors). *Establishes:* reluctant→earnest turn WITHOUT certainty —
    the middle path held at the midpoint.

### Movement IV — The name (now)
13. **The unread box.** Companion designations optional; he read only what was required
    (anchor content; plant-dependency note). The debt named in his ledger-idiom (a debt
    in a currency you cannot spend — family).
14. **He says a name into the alcove, off every camera.** Not the right one — there was
    no right one left. **Frame spend: it does not go in the record.** *Craft: the
    chapter's ONLY sub-4-word paragraphs cluster here; everything slows.*
15. **The press-and-ease. "I chose to read the easing as an answer."** (choice-verb
    fixed). Dual-coded to the last: an answer, or a pressure differential; never
    adjudicated.

### Movement V — Toward morning
16. **Curtain: the willingness.** The sweep comes in the morning — Adler, the rigs that
    cannot catch him, a floor of pilgrims who cannot hide, an auditor's finished curve —
    and he knows what he is going to do; he has known since a porter came up out of a
    loop; he needed the night, the listener, and the number at one to stop calling it
    something else. **Withhold the what.** Past-curtain licensed; end shape: quiet is
    EARNED here — the one chapter where the quiet close is the chosen option, not the
    default (9.5 — choose it on purpose, on the record).
