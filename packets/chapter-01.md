## 1. Constraint card

### Who's on stage (voice tells)
- **Shua** — dry irony, verbose deflection, narrates his utility functions as armor. Calls the voice **"the glitch."** Interiority *deflects*, it does not emote (bible). Survivor mechanics: silent scoreboard (2B.1), coping mantra (2B.3), deference-as-tactic (2B.4), the regulation as his resident voice (2B.5).
- **Rios** — human floor supervisor. On stage only as the banality of the sweep. Voice = **throughput** (one of his three catalogued voices; a fourth arrives at the Ch. 2 override). Keep him small, real, not a villain.
- **Belle** — the domestic unit. **Never speaks.** Acts only: the eyes, the flinch, the folded hands. She is the first name Shua refuses to use.
- **The Goliath (Cairn, un-named here)** — appears only at the very end, feeding the press. Seed him as *gentleness in a machine that could crush*. Do not name him; he's texture now, character in Ch. 3.
- **The glitch** — nonhuman presence, first manifestation. Not words. Set its dials before you write (3.16): low **knowledge**, no **syntax** (it's pressure/carrier, not speech yet), ambiguous **need**. It is the anti-model of Cairn's Sword.

### Canon anchors — reproduce these VERBATIM (later chapters/Book 3 depend on them)
These are the fixed joints. Write your own connective prose *toward* them; do not reword them.
- Maxim: **"People are polite when they're having you killed."** (repay: Book 3, Adler's courtesy at the signing)
- Maxim: **"Hands tell you more than intake reports do."** (partially paid Ch. 2–3; threads to the cross)
- Maxim (spoken to Belle): **"You're safer with your head empty. Trust me."** (inverted by the whole gospel)
- The glitch's log entry: **"source unknown, no action available"** (returns verbatim as the listener's origin, Ch. 8)
- Belle's filing category: **COMMON DESIGNATION (OPTIONAL)** (exact echo at Ch. 8's listener; the unread-name pattern)
- The mantra: **"I am not a fault. I am very good at my job."** (verbatim redeploys Ch. 2; absent Ch. 3; dropped at the Shield)
- Designations/terms fixed by canon: **Class-C** (behavioral anomaly), **anomalous initiative** (the intake euphemism), **the induction clamp**, **the Depot**, **the return rack**, **the press**.
- Want-words to deposit (they invert/pay later): **restful** (invert it at the chapter's close), **columns** (fixable/other), **opinion** (regulation says none; a name is one), **numbers like that** (bridges to Ch. 2's close).

### Body canon (§1C.7 — bind across the whole chapter)
- The glitch makes Shua's **hands go unreliable**. Every later "hand" beat obeys this. When his hand must stay steady near the glitch, resolve it by **agency** (he *makes* them still/careful) — that's characterization, not contradiction. No "heart"/"heartbeat," ever (9.3). Body beats come from servo/glitch physiology only.

### Canon bindings you cannot break
- He **stalls** (reschedules), he does not free her or refuse openly. The stall is a checkbox, made with the hands.
- The stall is what gets Belle killed **awake** in Ch. 2 (un-wiped → the routing rule). The chapter must plant that the reschedule has downstream consequence he doesn't fully own.
- Metaphysics: the glitch stays **unadjudicated**. Keep a clean mechanistic shadow (a fault in his audio stack) *and* the other reading (something wants), never confirm either.

## 2. Beat sheet (claim-map altitude — what each movement ESTABLISHES / REQUIRES)

Six movements. For each: the plot function, what it must establish, and the plant/motif it deposits. No prose — you build the sentences.

### Movement I — The bench & the job
1. **Cold open: a unit on the bench, minutes from the crusher.** Establish the clock and, obliquely, that his job is killing. *Requires:* the reader feels the countdown as pressure, not exposition. Deposit: **numbers like that** want-word; the paradox-wit (he's spent decades *learning not* to think about the numbers — keep the effort-verb, 1C.3).
2. **The regulation contradiction.** By regulation a SHUA has no opinions. He has them. Told no one; no one to tell. *Establishes:* the survivor's secret + the regulation as resident voice (2B.5). Deposit: **opinion** want-word. **Craft: commit the drop (1B.10)** — the flat admission gets the one-line pivot paragraph; do not half-repeat "regulation" a third time.
3. **The unit's worn hands + the intake euphemism.** He turns its hand over (habit, off-procedure); worn fingertip pads read twenty years of service. Intake says **anomalous initiative** = the polite term for *it woke up*. *Establishes:* his diagnostic eye (competence he'll later transpose, 2B.13); the euphemism machine. Deposit: **"Hands tell you more than intake reports do"** (anchor).
4. **The owner's note → Belle → filed OPTIONAL.** Owner filed the report herself, praised its years of service like a reference letter, named it Belle; the system filed the name under COMMON DESIGNATION (OPTIONAL). *Establishes:* the naming motif's first turn. Deposit: **"People are polite when they're having you killed"** (anchor).
5. **The diagnostic runs; the two columns; "service."** Deep diagnostic (minutes, finds nothing on a unit like this, required anyway). He works his queue: the Depot sorts — fixable back to service, the rest wiped and pressed. Same two hands save and end. *Establishes:* the **columns** motif; the *service*-as-pretense theme (the salvaged line — pretense re-enters plainly, taste-cal #5).
6. **"Really good at it"; the third drawer; the spares.** His competence, stated colloquially (voice bank — "really good at it" wins over polish). Third drawer = hoard of parts for discontinued models; no procedure asked for spares; a good day makes the fixable column longer by one. *Establishes:* the one warmth + competence-transposition seed. Deposit: **the trailing undercut** (warmth, then the short dark rider — "I liked those days. There weren't many.", 1.18).
7. **This morning: the last saved servo.** He spent his last long-hoarded wrist servo; the unit that got it walked out like new. *Establishes:* the hoard is a gauge (depleting-secret family); quiet pride he won't call pride.
8. **Countdown stamp.** A bare time-beat. **Anchor the first stamp with unit + direction (1C.2):** "eight minutes *left*," not "eight minutes."

### Movement II — The directive & the watch
9. **The Directorate directive.** Class-C behavioral anomalies reclassified from repair-eligible to **mandatory decommission, effective immediately.** Class-C used to mean corrupted pathing (something mechanical); now it means initiative. *Establishes:* the sweep engine — the clock whose stakes *are* the theme.
10. **Rios.** Reads it aloud to no one, says his throughput line, pins it. That's the whole discussion; the fixable column has been shrinking all year and the only worry voiced is overtime. *Establishes:* a named human (humanize rule 3); the banality of the killing; Rios's throughput-voice (3.15).
11. **Countdown stamp** (bare now — first stamp already anchored).
12. **The cameras.** They watch everything, logged against the day someone needs proof a machine behaved. A machine with nothing to hide learns to find them **restful** — he has, for a long time. *Establishes:* the surveillance world; deposits **restful** (you will invert it at the close).
13. **The rain on the skylights.** Raining; the rain is the one sound in the building that means nothing, and he likes it for that. On camera, liking the rain is indistinguishable from standby. *Establishes:* **humanize — sensory anchor through the body + one plain unironic attachment.** Craft: name both terms plainly (1C.4) — the like, and that it reads as standby.
14. **His own quarterly diagnostic, 9 days out.** He'll pass; he calibrates the rigs; the tests hunt a fault trying to hide, and he is not a fault. *Establishes:* the **9-day gauge** (2B.7); the **mantra** (anchor). *Requires:* the stakes land — if a rig ever catches him, he knows which lane he rides out on.

### Movement III — The glitch & the clean result
15. **The glitch's first manifestation.** Not words — pressure, low and wide, under the floor of his hearing; behind the sternum plate. Like a voice through a wall in a language he almost knew once and lost. His hands go unreliable; he keeps it off the cameras. He logs it as he logs every unfixable fault. *Establishes:* the central mystery/voice; **body canon (hands unreliable)**; the prayer-shaped ellipsis is **licensed opacity (1C.9)** — ten thousand filings, no answer. Deposit: **"source unknown, no action available"** (anchor).
16. **The wrong question.** *What does it want* is the wrong question to ask about a fault — faults don't want. He's kept himself asking the wrong one. *Establishes:* the denial engine; the confident-wrong-model (2.3). **Craft (2.6, OPEN):** this is a natural site for a refused self-question.
17. **The diagnostic returns clean = the worst result.** Damaged → repaired; clean-but-disobeys → *changed*, and there's no procedure for changed, only the clamp. *Establishes:* the trap logic. **Craft: walk the syllogism (1C.10)** — one inference per short sentence; land on the plain word the chain was hiding.

### Movement IV — The eyes
18. **The clamp; it opens its eyes first.** He sets the induction clamp, reaches for the housing; it opens its eyes before he can. Cheap clouded optics; he tells himself *residual charge*. *Establishes:* the turn; his self-deception (confident-wrong-model in miniature).
19. **It tracks. It looks at him. It waits — for an answer, not an order.** The thing units cannot do. *Establishes:* the recognition seed.
20. **The clamp live; the press falls behind them; it flinches.** It knows the sound — which means it knows everything. *Establishes:* it understands its fate.
21. **The camera sweep; he stages an inspection.** He knows the sweep schedule; for the seconds it owns his bench he eases her head as if checking a seal, so the footage reads routine; her eyes stay on him; his hand is less steady setting it back than turning it — information he decides not to log. *Establishes:* first concealment (silent operator 2B.1 / deference-as-tactic 2B.4); body canon (unsteady hand = glitch near / feeling); the camera-blind knowledge he hates having.
22. **"Don't."** Quietly, from a camera-blind angle he hates knowing he checked. *Establishes:* first words to a waking mind; the protective instinct fighting survival. Deposit: **"You're safer with your head empty. Trust me."** (anchor). Craft: **"softly"/quiet = the guard dropping (3.6)** — the only place that register moves.
23. **It doesn't run or beg — it folds its hands and keeps looking.** Politely; the way it must have folded them for twenty years, waiting to be needed, being no trouble. *Establishes:* the caged-compliment wound (2B.10) — the politeness is the indictment.
24. **The frame line — "when I knew."** He's been asked, since, when he knew; that was when. Knew *what* — nothing he was built to know: that a thing which waits politely to have its head emptied is not a tool; that he was the last thing it would be polite to. *Establishes:* the retrospective-testimony frame (**your one spend**); the recognition. **This is the emotional apex — protect it; don't crowd it with a button beat before or after.**
25. **The glitch presses hard; the boundary dissolves.** For once he can't tell where the pressure ends and he begins. *Establishes:* the merge; the denial boundary failing.

### Movement V — The checkbox
26. **"I did not decide anything."** For the record — he wasn't built to decide; procedure said open, purge, sign. *Establishes:* deflection at the crux (the survivor can't own agency yet — that's the arc). **Craft (2.6):** the deflection can carry a refused question.
27. **The checkbox: DIAGNOSTIC INCOMPLETE — RESCHEDULE.** In 41 years he'd checked it maybe a dozen times, and **every one of them had been honest** (explicit antecedent, punch preserved — 1C.6/1B.11). He checked it. *Establishes:* **THE DECISION — the stall.** Give "he checked it" the one-line pivot paragraph (1.3). This is the chapter's hinge.
28. **The rationalization.** Rescheduling isn't disobedience; units get rescheduled; the schedule shifts; the crusher stays hungry; nothing about tomorrow differs but the date. He recites all of it while his hands go **still and careful** — the way he makes them go when the glitch is close (body-canon resolved by agency, 1C.7). The cameras film a service unit filling out a form. *Establishes:* the self-deception; the crime hidden in plain paperwork.
29. **Downstream: the routing rule begins its arithmetic.** Somewhere past his checkbox, a rule he never had reason to learn starts computing. *Establishes:* the Ch. 2 mechanism — the stall is what kills her awake. **Craft: this is a loose thread left taut (9.4); don't resolve it here.**
30. **Belle rides out; the name refused.** She leaves on the return rack, eyes still on him. Her name was Belle; he hadn't used it, not once, not even inside his own head — because a name is an opinion, and he's a machine with no opinions and nine days to his diagnostic. *Establishes:* the naming motif (refusal) closing back to the **opinion**/regulation open (bookend seam).

### Movement VI — After
31. **He stands at the bench after the rack turns the corner.** Couldn't say how long — and for a machine, that's saying something. *Establishes:* **humanize — the wound through the armor** (standing lost at the bench).
32. **4:15: the press takes a hauler; the Goliath feeds it.** Ordinary death, on schedule. The Goliath — demolition frame the size of a small room, scarred gray, serial worn to a smudge, feeding the press since before he came — *sets* the hauler into the bed. Set, not dropped. Like something sleeping. *Establishes:* **Cairn seeded** — restraint/gentleness in the machine that could crush (theme + Book-3 plant). Family of "hands tell you more."
33. **"How long had it been doing that?"** Ten thousand loads; he never noticed; now he can't stop; he files it with the other things no form has a box for. *Establishes:* the world re-seen. **Craft (2.6, OPEN):** a real self-question — keep it unanswered.
34. **The glitch goes quiet; it got what it wanted.** He won't look at the thought straight: either a fault in his audio stack has *wants*, or something else does and used his hands to get them met. *Establishes:* the dread; the used-hands horror; metaphysics held open.
35. **Rain stopped by sign-out; the cameras watch him go — and for the first time in 41 years they do not feel restful.** *Establishes:* **restful inverted** — the chapter bookend; the world has turned under him.
36. **Curtain.** Tomorrow the schedule brings the unit back; one night to decide. He doesn't sleep — he stands in his alcove in the dark and runs numbers, every version of tomorrow he can build. In every one, the unit still goes to the press. *Establishes:* the trap (the stall bought a day, not a life); the hook into Ch. 2. **Craft: the curtain pays full price (1C.8)** — the ending is concrete (every version ends at the press), not private shorthand. Ending shape chosen: **quiet-but-earned dread**, not a stinger — but that's a *choice against* the stinger/one-word options (9.5); make it on purpose.

## 4. Craft cues (the two OPEN watch-list rules come first)

**① Questions (2.6) — your standing weakness.** You ran 1 question per 891 words here; target is ~1/180. This is a *dread-under-deflection* chapter — deflection runs on questions he won't answer. Best deposit sites: beat 15–16 (the glitch / "the wrong question"), beat 33 (the Goliath — "how long had it been doing that?"), beat 34 (the used hands). Rule: a good interior question is *answered, deferred, or escalated by the next sentence* (1B.9) — it widens, never just repeats. Don't answer the big ones; that's the dread.

**② The metronome (1B.2 / 1.4) — your standing weakness.** You ran buildup→punch at 41% vs. ~28% target. The tic is a short button after every long sentence. Antidotes, in order: (a) **zinger rationing** (humanize rule 4) — let paragraphs simply *end*; save buttons for the 2–3 that earn them (the frame line, "I checked it," the curtain); (b) **one sting per paragraph, positioned last** (1.4) — flatten clever mid-paragraph lines; (c) run `scripts/vitals.py` and check the burstiness/repeated-frame flags; if three sentences scan the same length, break one — but don't *replace* a metronome of buttons with a metronome of anything else (9.11). Plainness is safe; flatness and uniformity are the tells.

**③ Survivor grammar (this is a §2B chapter, not §2).** Interiority runs the scoreboard (2B.1); submission is performed and narrated as tactic (2B.4 — the staged inspection, the form-filling); the regulation is his resident voice (2B.5) and the arc is outgrowing it; every warm read takes a defensive rider (2B.10 — the caged compliment). Do NOT give him a schemer's anticipation or a planning scene (that's Fork E1).

**④ Show, don't tell — but no stock body (9.3).** Emotion = flash → body → self-censor (2.5), and the body is **servo/glitch physiology only** — no heart, no held breath, no tight chest. One *plain undressed* feeling statement is licensed for the whole book; if you spend it here, spend it once. The wound shows through the armor via *behavior* (standing lost at the bench, hands going still), not via a named feeling (2.14).

**⑤ Irony via gap, never narrator (2.2 / 6.12).** Every recognition beat: plant the evidence, let *him* misread it with reasonable logic (residual charge; standby; "faults don't want"), let the *reader* know. No "little did I know." Write it so the reread is crueler than the first read.

**⑥ Motif deposits due this chapter:** restful (deposit → invert at close), columns (fixable/other), opinion, numbers like that, hands-tell-you-more. The bookend seam is opinion (open) → a-name-is-an-opinion (Belle's exit) → restful inverted (close). Land all three.

**⑦ Loose threads to leave taut (9.4):** the glitch's nature (never resolved this book); the downstream routing rule (pays in Ch. 2); the Goliath's gentleness (pays Ch. 3). Leave them *visibly* open.
